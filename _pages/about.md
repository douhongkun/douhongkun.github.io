I am **Hongkun Dou (窦泓焜)**, an Assistant Professor at [Zhongguancun Academy](https://www.bza.edu.cn/) (from July 2026). I received my Ph.D. in Pattern Recognition and Intelligent Systems from [Beihang University](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/) (2021–2026), advised by Prof. [Yue Deng](https://shi.buaa.edu.cn/yuedeng/zh_CN/index.htm), and my B.Eng. in Aircraft Control and Information Engineering from Beihang University (2017–2021).

My research focuses on **deep generative models**—especially diffusion and flow models—for controllable generation, inverse problem solving, reward alignment, and **post-training / enhancement of diffusion large language models**, as well as applications in **AI for Science** (e.g., meteorological field reconstruction and DNA/protein sequence design).

Research Interests
======
- **Controllable generation & alignment of deep generative models**: diffusion models, flow models, guided generation for images/videos, inverse problems, target-guided generation, and reward alignment.
- **Diffusion large language models**: post-training, controllable generation, and capability enhancement for diffusion-based LLMs.
- **Generative models for AI for Science**: sparse reconstruction and assimilation of meteorological data; design and optimization of functional DNA and protein sequences.

Selected Highlights
======
- First-author papers in **IEEE TPAMI**, **IEEE TIP**, **IEEE TNNLS**, **ICML**, **ICLR**, **AAAI**, etc.
- Collaboration with **Huawei 2012 Labs** on generative AI for meteorological reconstruction and forecasting (integrated into the Pangu weather model).
- Reviewer / PC member for ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, AAAI, CVPR, ECCV.
